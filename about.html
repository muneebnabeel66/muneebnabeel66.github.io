About me

I am a data scientist with an MSc in Data Science (Distinction) from London South Bank University and a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Central Punjab.

I enjoy the full journey of a data problem: extracting and cleaning raw data, designing how it is stored, building and evaluating models, and explaining the results in plain language. I follow the CRISP-DM methodology so every project starts with a clear business question and ends with a recommendation.

My work spans healthcare forecasting, public-sector incident analysis, renewable energy and historical research data.
