The problem

Plan around the weather

Solar output depends on the weather, which makes supply hard to plan. The goal was to predict system production from conditions such as radiation, sunshine, temperature and humidity.

The data

One year, every hour

8,760 hourly records with wind speed, sunshine, air pressure, radiation, air temperature, relative humidity and system production. Exploration showed no output at night, a summer peak from June to August and a strong link between radiation and production.

Approach

Explore, then compare models

  1. Explore. Checked distributions, outliers with IQR, correlations and multicollinearity with VIF (all below 5).
  2. Engineer. Extracted year, month, day and hour from timestamps.
  3. Prepare. Split 70/30 into train and test sets and standardised features.
  4. Model. Trained a Random Forest pipeline with SelectKBest and a Linear Regression baseline.
  5. Evaluate. Compared R², mean absolute error and error distributions.
Results

Next steps

What I would improve

Lag and shift features and time-series models such as ARIMA would capture the daily and seasonal cycle more directly.
